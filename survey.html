How this sample survey is weighted

What matters operationally: overall satisfaction and willingness to recommend make up half of the sample score. The rest is driven by the sales consultant, finance/paperwork, feature explanation and delivery condition. That means survey protection starts during the sale and delivery, not when the email arrives.

Follow-up rhythm

Within 24 hours
Check the vehicle, feature setup and anything promised at delivery.
Next few days
Give a light survey heads-up and ask if anything still needs attention.
If survey arrives
Help them recognize it. One clean reminder is enough.
If there is a problem
Stop talking about the survey. Clarify the issue and work the recovery.
After completion
Thank them and go back to ownership care. Do not ask what score they gave.

Internal next step

Do not coach the score: do not ask for “all 10s,” tell the customer a lower score hurts you, tell them which boxes to mark or ask them to hold the survey until they can score it a certain way. Ask for honest feedback and fix what you can.
Issue recovery: if the customer raises a real problem, the survey stops being the conversation. Clarify exactly what happened, isolate whether anything else is wrong, involve the right person and give the customer a clear next step. Do not promise a finance correction, payment change, reimbursement or other outcome you have not verified.
